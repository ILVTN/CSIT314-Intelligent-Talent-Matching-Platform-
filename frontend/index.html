How Intelligent Talent Matching Platform Works

Simple tools for candidates and employers in one platform.

For Candidates

Free users can create a profile, browse available jobs, search by keyword, and apply for suitable job opportunities.

Membership users can receive recommended job matches based on their skills, education, experience, location, and work preferences.

For Employers

Free users can create a company profile, post job vacancies, search candidates, and review job applications.

Membership users can receive recommended candidates based on job requirements, candidate skills, education, experience, location, and work preferences.

Smart Matching

The system compares skills, education, experience, work mode, and location to find suitable candidate-job matches for membership users.
